Learn the Science

Scientific Explanations & Glossary

Understand the biology driving your hypertrophy and fat loss.

🧬 Progressive Hypertrophy

To build muscle while losing fat, gym lifts must push fibers near failure. Over 10 weeks, aim to add reps or weight every session.

🔥 EPOC — Afterburn Effect

Excess Post-Exercise Oxygen Consumption. High-intensity training (T25) keeps metabolic rate elevated up to 24h post-workout, maximizing fat oxidation.

🫀 Zone 2 Aerobic Conditioning

Running at 60–70% max heart rate teaches mitochondria to burn fat as primary fuel. Improves stamina and recovery during heavy gym sessions.

⚖️ BMI Composition Balance

This program lowers fat mass while building skeletal muscle, improving your BMI composition without sacrificing strength.

🌾 Mediterranean Glycemic Index

Complex carbs (quinoa, farro) provide slow glucose absorption, avoiding insulin spikes and directing nutrients to recovering muscle tissue.

💧 Cellular Rehydration

T25 and heavy lifting deplete glycogen and water. Electrolytes force nutrients into cell walls, optimizing protein synthesis and hypertrophy.

🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection

Consciously focusing attention on the working muscle increases motor-unit recruitment and EMG activity, producing more complete fiber stimulation and better hypertrophy per rep.

😴 Sleep & Recovery Science

Deep slow-wave sleep triggers the largest pulse of growth hormone, while REM consolidates motor learning. Aim for 7–9 hours to repair micro-tears and replenish glycogen.

⚛️ ATP Energy Pathways

Short sprints draw on the phosphocreatine system, T25 intervals tap glycolysis, and Zone 2 runs rely on aerobic oxidation. Training all three widens your total energy capacity.
