How to Setup Your Tech Stack

Combine NRC, NTC, FitOn Pro, and Beachbody/BODi to run seamlessly concurrently.

🏃‍♂️

Nike Run Club

Open NRC before heading out. For Saturday runs, select "Guided Runs" > "Time-Based" or "Easy Runs". This promotes Zone 2 recovery. For Tuesdays, select "Speed Run" (Fartleks or Intervals).

🏋️‍♀️

Gym Upper Body

To construct a premium look, target major muscle hubs. Apply Progressive Overload: Track your weights in notes. Focus on Upper Push (chest/shoulders) Mon, Upper Pull (back/biceps) Wed, and Core/Arms Fri.

🧘

FitOn Pro

Your FitOn Pro plan focuses on mobility. After heavy running or heavy gym sessions, open FitOn and load a "10-15 Min Deep Stretch" or "Core Yoga Flow". Use this to rapidly flush lactic acid from muscle fibers.

⏱️

BODi / T25

T25 is an athletic sprint. Keep transitions tight. Follow Shaun T's strict scheduling: Weeks 1-5 use Alpha (no equipment). Weeks 6-8 use Beta (light weights). Weeks 9-10 shift to Gamma (strength & speed overload).

Learn the Science of Your Body

Scientific Explanations & Glossary

Understand the biological pathways driving your muscle hypertrophy and healthy BMI balance.

🧬 Progressive Hypertrophy

To stimulate muscle growth while losing fat, your gym lifts must force fibers near their threshold. Over the 10 weeks, aim to lift heavier or complete more clean repetitions on every single session.

🔥 EPOC (Afterburn Effect)

Excess Post-Exercise Oxygen Consumption. High-intensity cardiorespiratory training (like T25) keeps your metabolic resting rate raised for up to 24 hours post-workout, maximizing daily fat oxidation.

🫀 Zone 2 Aerobic Conditioning

Running at 60-70% max heart rate teaches your mitochondria to utilize fat lipids as primary energy. This improves your systemic stamina, ensuring you recover fast during hard gym lifting.

⚖️ BMI Composition Balance

Traditional BMI doesn't differentiate fat from muscle. This hybrid program optimizes your BMI by lowering body fat mass while building skeletal muscle structure, giving you a dense, athletic constitution.

🌾 Mediterranean Glycemic Index

Consuming clean complex carbs (quinoa, farro) ensures slow glucose absorption. This avoids high insulin spikes, guiding nutrients directly to recovering muscles rather than storing them in adipocytes (fat cells).

💧 Cellular Rehydration Ratio

Intense sweating under T25 and heavy lifting depletes glycogen and water storage. Electrolytes and water are mandatory to force nutrients into the cell walls, optimizing hypertrophy.
